## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective of this project is to analyze retail transaction data, uncover revenue and profitability drivers, evaluate regional and product-category performance, and provide actionable insights to optimize business operations and sales strategy. Incorporated data analysis techniques, specializing in time series analysis, to deliver valuable insights, accurate sales forecasting, and interactive dashboard creation, driving business success.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
* **Track Core KPIs:** Monitor Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Orders, and Average Order Value (AOV).
* **Trend & Seasonality Analysis:** Identify monthly, quarterly, and year-over-year (YoY) growth patterns.
* **Category & Product Performance:** Pinpoint top-performing sub-categories and identify loss-making products.
* **Regional & Customer Segmentation:** Analyze geographic distribution of sales and evaluate customer segment behavior (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).
* **Shipping & Operational Efficiency:** Assess delivery timelines and the impact of shipping modes and discounts on profit margins.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard visualization.
* **Power Query (M):** Data cleaning, transformation, handling missing values, and formatting data types.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for YoY growth, running totals, profit margins, and dynamic rankings.
* **Data Source:** Superstore / Retail Sales Dataset (`.csv` / `.xlsx`).
